## 7. International Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy & Export Costing Architecture

### 7.1 Statutory Export Clearance & GST Mechanism (HSN 8471)
1. **Zero-Rated Export Supply (Section 16 IGST Act, 2017):**
   - Dgen Technologies executes Form **GST RFD-11 (Letter of Undertaking / LUT)** on the GST Common Portal before commencing international export shipments.
   - All physical units clear Kolkata Air Cargo Complex (CCU) at **0% IGST**.
2. **Rule 89(4) Input Tax Credit (ITC) Cash Refund:**
   - Unutilized domestic GST paid on raw components (Section A electronics, Section B plastics/fasteners, Section C packaging) totaling **₹850 to ₹1,100 per unit** is claimed as a direct cash refund under **Rule 89(4) of the CGST Rules, 2017**.
   - The statutory 1.5x turnover cap ($1.5 	imes ₹19,068 = ₹28,602$) easily accommodates the $248 – $320 export invoice price.
3. **Mandatory BIS CRS & WPC Export Exemptions:**
   - **BIS CRS Registration Exemption:** Under **DGFT Foreign Trade Policy (FTP)** and **MeitY Circular No. 01 of 2014 / Notification No. 13/2012-IT**, goods manufactured in India exclusively for export are **100% exempt from BIS Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)**.
   - **WPC ETA Exemption:** Wireless products manufactured strictly for export are exempt from domestic WPC marketing licenses.

### 7.3 International Payment Gateway & Merchant of Record (MoR)
- **Recommended Strategy: Merchant of Record (Paddle / Lemon Squeezy):**
  - **Fee:** **5.0% + $0.50** per transaction.
  - **Legal Reseller Status:** The MoR assumes 100% legal tax liability. Automatically collects and remits EU VAT, UK VAT, and US State Sales Tax, eliminating the need for Dgen to file foreign tax returns across 50 US states and 27 EU nations.
  - **Alternative (Stripe International):** 3.99% + $0.30 + 2% FX fee + heavy administrative burden of filing RBI purpose codes (P0103) and manual e-BRC tracking.

### 7.4 Customs Duties, De Minimis Thresholds & Regional VAT
1. **United States (Section 321 De Minimis):**
   - Under **19 U.S.C. § 1321**, shipments valued under **$800.00 USD** enter the US **100% duty-free and tax-free**.
   - ADAM ($248 – $320) incurs **$0.00 customs duty** and **$0.00 federal tax** on arrival.
2. **European Union (WTO ITA & VAT):**
   - Under the WTO Information Technology Agreement, computers and microcomputers under **HSN 8471** have **0.0% customs duty**.
   - Consignments >€150 require standard import VAT (19%–22%), collected upfront at checkout via MoR and shipped **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** to guarantee seamless doorstep delivery.
3. **United Kingdom (UKGT & Post-Brexit VAT):**
   - **0.0% customs duty** under the UK Global Tariff for HSN 8471. Import VAT (20%) collected at checkout for shipments >£135.
